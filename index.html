Saturday · Venice to West LA on foot

Saturday Westside Crawl

Four ways to walk from Wally's place in Venice to Max and Eric's place in West LA. Leaving at 1 PM, each route has 14 or 15 bars on the way, and you can add or drop bars on any of them. Each route is one start plus one finish: the Boardwalk or Lincoln start, then the Wilshire or Sawtelle finish, meeting at Jameson's on Main St. Most walks between bars run 5 to 20 min, and every route adjusts to when you leave and how long you stay at each bar. You can also start and end anywhere else on the list, in either direction.

The routes and the map only show when this page is opened in a regular web browser.

Friend stops

Pick any home or bar as the start and the end, and the routes fill in the bars between them, either direction. Change anything and the routes update. If a bar would be closed when you get there, the route swaps in a nearby spot that's open or skips it, and anything it can't fix shows in red. A friend's place gets the same time as a bar and goes on any route that passes it, and so does Wally's place or Max and Eric's place when it isn't the start or the end. The best pick includes the friend stops you switched on, then has the most bars, then the fewest walks over 20 min.

Pick a route

Use the plus and minus on any route to add or drop bars. Plus adds the best ranked bar and minus drops the lowest ranked one, using the stars under Rank the bars, unless that would add a walk over 20 min. When the stars don't decide it, plus adds the bar that best splits up a long walk and minus drops the one that leaves the walks shortest and most even. It only picks bars that are open when you get there.

Rank the bars
