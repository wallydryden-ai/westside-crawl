Saturday Westside Crawl

Four ways to walk from Wally's place in Venice to Max and Eric's place in West LA, with up to 15 bars on the way. Each route is one start plus one finish: the Boardwalk or Lincoln start, then the Wilshire or Sawtelle finish, meeting at Jameson's on Main St. Most walks between bars run 5 to 20 min, and every route adjusts to when you leave and how long you stay at each bar.

This is the plan for leaving Wally's place at 1:00 PM, with 20 min at each bar and no friend stops. The leave time, bar time and friend switches only work when the page is opened in a web browser.
