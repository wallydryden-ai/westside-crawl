Saturday Westside Crawl

Four ways to walk from Wally's place in Venice to Max and Eric's place in West LA. Leaving at 1 PM, each route has 14 or 15 bars on the way, and you can add or drop bars on any of them. Each route is one start plus one finish: the Boardwalk or Lincoln start, then the Wilshire or Sawtelle finish, meeting at Jameson's on Main St. Most walks between bars run 5 to 20 min, and every route adjusts to when you leave and how long you stay at each bar.

This is the plan for leaving Wally's place at 1:00 PM, with 20 min at each bar and no friend stops. The leave time, bar time and friend switches only work when the page is opened in a web browser.

Friend stops on routes 1 and 3

Change any of these and all four routes update. If a bar would be closed when you get there, the route swaps in a nearby spot that's open or skips it, and anything it can't fix shows in red. A friend's place gets the same time as a bar. The best pick includes any friend stops you switched on, then has the most bars, then the fewest walks over 20 min.

Pick a route

Use the plus and minus on any route to add or drop bars. It adds the bar that best splits up a long walk and drops the one that leaves the walks shortest and most even, and it only picks bars that are open when you get there.
